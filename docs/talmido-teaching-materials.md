# Talmido teaching materials

Open `/talmido/{chapter}?view=slides` for chapters 6–10, or select **Teaching slides** inside a lesson. Each deck includes a classroom discussion, recap quiz and answers, and speaker notes. The viewer supports previous/next, arrow keys while the slide has focus, a slide selector, readable text, and expandable teacher notes.

- [Chapter 6: Holy Priesthood](../public/resources/talmido/chapter-6-holy-priesthood.pptx): 10 slides.
- [Chapter 7: The Living Sacrifice](../public/resources/talmido/chapter-7-the-living-sacrifice-detailed.pptx): 22 detailed teaching slides.
- [Chapter 8: Holy Confession](../public/resources/talmido/chapter-8-holy-confession-detailed.pptx): 15 detailed teaching slides.
- [Chapter 9: Anointing of the Sick](../public/resources/talmido/chapter-9-anointing-of-the-sick.pptx): 10 slides.
- [Chapter 10: A Christian Home](../public/resources/talmido/chapter-10-a-christian-home.pptx): 10 slides.

- [Chapters 6–10 practice questions and answers](../public/resources/talmido/chapters-6-10-quiz-and-answers.pdf): six questions per chapter, with teacher answers and textbook page references.

Based on the supplied Our Sacramental Life, Sixth Grade, June 2026 curriculum from the Sunday School of the North American Dioceses of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church. Summaries and practice questions are paraphrased teaching aids. Images are from the supplied curriculum and credited in speaker notes. Questions remain separate from Mark Challenge.

The slide manifest is `data/talmido-slides.json`; preview images and PowerPoint downloads are under `public/resources/talmido`.

Chapters 7 and 8 use a light reading layout with complete explanations directly on each slide. The app renders their paragraphs as responsive, selectable text. Their detailed PowerPoint downloads use the same teaching content. The earlier brief versions remain available at their original asset URLs for existing links.
