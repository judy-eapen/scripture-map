# Talmido teaching materials

- [Chapter 6: Holy Priesthood](../public/resources/talmido/chapter-6-holy-priesthood.pptx): editable ten-slide sample lesson with teacher notes, discussion and a recap quiz.
- [Chapters 6–10 practice questions and answers](../public/resources/talmido/chapters-6-10-quiz-and-answers.pdf): six questions per chapter, thirty questions total. Student pages come first, followed by the teacher answer key and printed textbook page references.

These materials summarize the supplied *Our Sacramental Life*, Sixth Grade, June 2026 curriculum published by the Sunday School of the North American Dioceses of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church. The questions were newly written at the user's request and have not been added to the application's quiz bank. Chapters 7–10 slide decks are not included in this sample.

After deployment, the downloads are available at `/resources/talmido/chapter-6-holy-priesthood.pptx` and `/resources/talmido/chapters-6-10-quiz-and-answers.pdf`.
