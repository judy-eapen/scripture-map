# MMVS Bible Quiz — reference questions (from Judy's document)

Each chapter bank must cover every fact below with at least one question (any type). These are the facts the real quiz is likely to ask.


## 1 Kings

### 1 Kings 1
- King David was old and could not keep warm no matter how many clothes covered him. Who was brought in to care for him? — Abishag the Shunammite (1 Kings 1:3-4)
- Which of David’s sons exalted himself saying “I will be king,” and got ready chariots, horsemen, and fifty men to run before him? — Adonijah, the son of Haggith (1 Kings 1:5)
- Adonijah won over two important men to his side. Who were they? — Joab the son of Zeruiah (the army commander) and Abiathar the priest (1 Kings 1:7)
- On whose mule did Solomon ride down to Gihon to be anointed king? — King David’s own mule (1 Kings 1:33, 38)
- Who took the horn of oil from the tent and anointed Solomon? — Zadok the priest (1 Kings 1:39)
- When Adonijah heard Solomon was king, he was afraid. Where did he run and what did he take hold of? — He caught hold of the horns of the altar (1 Kings 1:50)

### 1 Kings 2
- Fill in the blank from David’s last charge to Solomon: “Be strong, and show yourself a ______.” — man (1 Kings 2:2)
- How many years did David reign in all, and how were those years divided? — Forty years: seven in Hebron and thirty-three in Jerusalem (1 Kings 2:11)
- What request did Adonijah make through Bathsheba that ended up costing him his life? — He asked for Abishag the Shunammite as his wife (1 Kings 2:17, 25)
- Solomon expelled Abiathar from the priesthood. Whose house did this fulfill the word of the LORD against? — The house of Eli in Shiloh (1 Kings 2:27)
- Where was Joab when Benaiah struck him down? — At the tent of the LORD, holding the horns of the altar (1 Kings 2:28-34)
- Solomon told Shimei he could live if he stayed in Jerusalem. What boundary was he never to cross? — The brook Kidron (1 Kings 2:37)
- Why did Shimei finally leave Jerusalem, breaking his oath? — Two of his slaves ran away to Achish king of Gath, and he went to bring them back (1 Kings 2:39-40)

### 1 Kings 3
- Solomon made a marriage alliance with which foreign king by marrying his daughter? — Pharaoh, king of Egypt (1 Kings 3:1)
- At which place did the LORD appear to Solomon in a dream by night, saying “Ask what I shall give you”? — Gibeon (1 Kings 3:5)
- What did Solomon ask God for? — An understanding mind to govern the people, to discern between good and evil (1 Kings 3:9)
- Name two things God gave Solomon that he had NOT asked for. — Riches and honor (1 Kings 3:13)
- In the case of the two women who both claimed the living baby, what did Solomon order to be brought to him? — A sword (1 Kings 3:24)
- How did Solomon know which woman was the real mother? — She begged him to give the child to the other woman rather than slay it, because her heart yearned for her son (1 Kings 3:26-27)

### 1 Kings 4
- Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel. What was each one’s duty? — To provide food for the king and his household, each man for one month in the year (1 Kings 4:7)
- How many proverbs did Solomon utter, and how many songs did he write? — Three thousand proverbs and a thousand and five songs (1 Kings 4:32)
- Complete this picture of peace in Solomon’s days: “Judah and Israel dwelt in safety, from Dan even to Beer-sheba, every man under his ______ and under his ______.” — vine … fig tree (1 Kings 4:25)
- Solomon spoke about trees “from the cedar that is in Lebanon” down to which small plant that grows out of the wall? — The hyssop (1 Kings 4:33)

### 1 Kings 5
- Which king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon, because he had always loved David? — Hiram (1 Kings 5:1)
- Why had David not been able to build a house for the name of the LORD? — Because of the warfare surrounding him on every side (1 Kings 5:3)
- What did Solomon give Hiram every year in exchange for the timber? — Twenty thousand cors of wheat and twenty thousand cors of beaten oil (1 Kings 5:11)
- Solomon raised a levy of forced labor of thirty thousand men. Who was in charge of the levy? — Adoniram (1 Kings 5:13-14)

### 1 Kings 6
- In which year after Israel came out of Egypt, and in which year of Solomon’s reign, did he begin to build the house of the LORD? — In the 480th year after the exodus, the fourth year of his reign (1 Kings 6:1)
- Give the dimensions of the house Solomon built for the LORD. — Sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, thirty cubits high (1 Kings 6:2)
- What sound was NOT heard in the temple while it was being built, and why? — No hammer, axe, or any tool of iron was heard, because the stone was prepared at the quarry (1 Kings 6:7)
- The two cherubim in the inner sanctuary were made of what wood, and how tall were they? — Olivewood, ten cubits high (1 Kings 6:23)
- How many years did Solomon take to build the house of the LORD? — Seven years (1 Kings 6:38)

### 1 Kings 7
- Solomon spent seven years building the LORD’s house. How long did he spend building his own house? — Thirteen years (1 Kings 7:1)
- Hiram, the skilled bronze worker from Tyre, was the son of a widow from which tribe? — Naphtali (1 Kings 7:14)
- What names were given to the two bronze pillars set up at the vestibule of the temple? — Jachin (the south pillar) and Boaz (the north pillar) (1 Kings 7:21)
- The molten sea stood upon twelve bronze oxen. How were the oxen arranged? — Three facing north, three west, three south, and three east (1 Kings 7:25)
- Why did Solomon leave all the temple vessels unweighed? — There were so many of them that the weight of the bronze could not be found out (1 Kings 7:47)

### 1 Kings 8
- When the priests brought the ark into the most holy place, what two things were inside it? — Nothing except the two tables of stone which Moses put there at Horeb (1 Kings 8:9)
- Why could the priests not stand to minister after they came out of the holy place? — A cloud filled the house — the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD (1 Kings 8:10-11)
- In his prayer of dedication, Solomon asked: “But will God indeed dwell on the earth?” What did he say could not contain God? — Heaven and the highest heaven (1 Kings 8:27)
- How many oxen and sheep did Solomon offer as peace offerings when the house of the LORD was dedicated? — Twenty-two thousand oxen and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep (1 Kings 8:63)
- When Solomon sent the people home on the eighth day, how did they go to their homes? — Joyful and glad of heart for all the goodness the LORD had shown to David and to Israel (1 Kings 8:66)

### 1 Kings 9
- When the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, what did He say He had done for the house Solomon built? — Consecrated it and put His name there forever; “my eyes and my heart will be there for all time” (1 Kings 9:3)
- Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee, but Hiram was not pleased with them. What name were they given? — The land of Cabul (1 Kings 9:11-13)
- Where did King Solomon build his fleet of ships? — At Ezion-geber, near Eloth on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom (1 Kings 9:26)
- The fleet went to Ophir and brought back gold. How much? — Four hundred and twenty talents (1 Kings 9:28)

### 1 Kings 10
- Why did the queen of Sheba come to Jerusalem? — She heard of Solomon’s fame concerning the name of the LORD and came to test him with hard questions (1 Kings 10:1)
- Finish the queen of Sheba’s words: “I did not believe the reports until I came and my own eyes had seen it; and behold, the ______ was not told me.” — half (1 Kings 10:7)
- How much gold came to Solomon in one year? — Six hundred and sixty-six talents (1 Kings 10:14)
- Describe Solomon’s great throne. — A throne of ivory overlaid with finest gold, with six steps, a calf’s head at the back, two lions beside the arm rests, and twelve lions on the steps (1 Kings 10:18-20)
- Once every three years, the fleet of ships of Tarshish came bringing five kinds of cargo. Name them. — Gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks (1 Kings 10:22)
- In Solomon’s days, silver was made as common in Jerusalem as what? — Stone (1 Kings 10:27)

### 1 Kings 11
- How many wives and concubines did Solomon have? — Seven hundred wives, princesses, and three hundred concubines (1 Kings 11:3)
- Solomon went after Ashtoreth, the goddess of which people? — The Sidonians (1 Kings 11:5)
- Name the two adversaries the LORD raised up against Solomon. — Hadad the Edomite and Rezon the son of Eliada (1 Kings 11:14, 23)
- What did the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite do with his new garment when he met Jeroboam on the road? — Tore it into twelve pieces and told Jeroboam to take ten (1 Kings 11:29-31)
- Why did God leave one tribe for Solomon’s son? — For the sake of David His servant and for the sake of Jerusalem, so David would always have a lamp before Him in Jerusalem (1 Kings 11:32, 36)
- When Solomon sought to kill Jeroboam, where did Jeroboam flee, and to whom? — To Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt (1 Kings 11:40)

### 1 Kings 12
- Where did all Israel gather to make Rehoboam king? — Shechem (1 Kings 12:1)
- What request did the people bring to Rehoboam? — “Your father made our yoke heavy; now lighten the hard service and heavy yoke, and we will serve you” (1 Kings 12:4)
- Rehoboam got advice from two groups. Whose counsel did he take? — He forsook the counsel of the old men and followed the young men who had grown up with him (1 Kings 12:8, 13-14)
- Complete Rehoboam’s harsh answer: “My father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with ______.” — scorpions (1 Kings 12:14)
- Who did Rehoboam send to the people of Israel, only for him to be stoned to death? — Adoram, the taskmaster over the forced labor (1 Kings 12:18)
- Which man of God told Rehoboam and Judah not to fight against their kinsmen Israel? — Shemaiah (1 Kings 12:22-24)
- Jeroboam made two calves of gold so the people wouldn’t go to Jerusalem. Where did he place them? — One in Bethel and the other in Dan (1 Kings 12:28-29)

### 1 Kings 13
- The man of God from Judah cried against the altar at Bethel and named a future king who would defile it. Who? — Josiah, a son to be born to the house of David (1 Kings 13:2)
- What happened to Jeroboam’s hand when he stretched it out saying “Lay hold of him”? — It dried up so he could not draw it back; it was restored when the man of God prayed for him (1 Kings 13:4-6)
- What three things had the LORD commanded the man of God from Judah not to do? — Not to eat bread, not to drink water, and not to return by the way he came (1 Kings 13:9)
- How did the old prophet of Bethel persuade the man of God to come back and eat with him? — He lied, claiming an angel had spoken to him by the word of the LORD (1 Kings 13:18)
- What met the man of God on the road and killed him? — A lion — and strangely, it did not eat the body or tear the donkey (1 Kings 13:24, 28)
- What instruction did the old prophet give his sons about his own burial? — “When I die, bury me in the grave in which the man of God is buried; lay my bones beside his bones” (1 Kings 13:31)

### 1 Kings 14
- When Jeroboam’s son Abijah fell sick, what did Jeroboam tell his wife to do before going to the prophet Ahijah? — Disguise herself so no one would know she was Jeroboam’s wife (1 Kings 14:2)
- Ahijah could not see because of old age, yet he knew who was at his door. How? — The LORD had told him the wife of Jeroboam was coming, and he recognized the sound of her feet (1 Kings 14:4-6)
- Why was the child Abijah the only one of Jeroboam’s house to be buried and mourned? — Because in him there was found something pleasing to the LORD, the God of Israel (1 Kings 14:13)
- In the fifth year of Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt attacked Jerusalem and took away the gold shields Solomon had made. What did Rehoboam make in their place? — Shields of bronze (1 Kings 14:25-27)

### 1 Kings 15
- Even though Abijam walked in sin, why did the LORD give him a lamp in Jerusalem? — For David’s sake, because David did what was right all his days, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite (1 Kings 15:4-5)
- What did King Asa do to his own mother Maacah, and why? — He removed her from being queen mother because she had made an abominable image for Asherah; he cut it down and burned it at the brook Kidron (1 Kings 15:13)
- When Baasha built Ramah to shut Judah in, what did Asa do? — He sent the silver and gold left in the temple and palace treasuries to Ben-hadad king of Syria, asking him to break his league with Baasha (1 Kings 15:18-19)
- What disease troubled Asa in his old age? — He was diseased in his feet (1 Kings 15:23)
- Who conspired against Nadab, killed him at Gibbethon, and then destroyed the whole house of Jeroboam? — Baasha the son of Ahijah (1 Kings 15:27-29)

### 1 Kings 16
- Which prophet carried the word of the LORD against Baasha? — Jehu the son of Hanani (1 Kings 16:1)
- What was King Elah doing when his servant Zimri struck him down? — Drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza at Tirzah (1 Kings 16:9-10)
- How long did Zimri reign, and how did his reign end? — Seven days; when the city was taken he burned the king’s house down over himself and died (1 Kings 16:15-18)
- From whom did Omri buy the hill of Samaria, and for how much? — From Shemer, for two talents of silver (1 Kings 16:24)
- Who was Ahab’s wife, and whose daughter was she? — Jezebel, the daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians (1 Kings 16:31)
- Hiel of Bethel rebuilt Jericho. What did it cost him? — The lives of his sons — the foundation at the cost of Abiram his first-born, and the gates at the cost of Segub his youngest (1 Kings 16:34)

### 1 Kings 17
- Elijah the Tishbite told Ahab there would be neither dew nor rain. For how long? — “These years, except by my word” (1 Kings 17:1)
- At the brook Cherith, who brought Elijah bread and meat in the morning and in the evening? — The ravens, as the LORD had commanded (1 Kings 17:4-6)
- What was the widow of Zarephath doing when Elijah first saw her at the gate of the city? — Gathering sticks (1 Kings 17:10)
- What promise did Elijah give the widow about her jar of meal and cruse of oil? — “The jar of meal shall not be spent, and the cruse of oil shall not fail, until the day that the LORD sends rain upon the earth” (1 Kings 17:14)
- When the widow’s son died, what did Elijah do in the upper chamber? — He stretched himself upon the child three times and cried to the LORD, and the child’s soul came into him again (1 Kings 17:21-22)

### 1 Kings 18
- During the famine, Obadiah hid a hundred prophets of the LORD from Jezebel. How and where? — By fifties in a cave, feeding them bread and water (1 Kings 18:4)
- What did Ahab call Elijah when they met, and how did Elijah answer? — “Is it you, you troubler of Israel?” Elijah replied that Ahab and his father’s house were the real troublers, for forsaking the LORD’s commandments and following the Baals (1 Kings 18:17-18)
- How many prophets of Baal and prophets of Asherah ate at Jezebel’s table? — Four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and four hundred prophets of Asherah (1 Kings 18:19)
- What challenge did Elijah put before the people on Mount Carmel? — “How long will you go limping with two different opinions? If the LORD is God, follow him; but if Baal, then follow him” (1 Kings 18:21)
- How many jars of water were poured on Elijah’s sacrifice, and how many times? — Four jars, three times over (1 Kings 18:33-34)
- When the fire of the LORD fell, what all did it consume? — The burnt offering, the wood, the stones, the dust, and it licked up the water in the trench (1 Kings 18:38)
- What did Elijah’s servant see on his seventh trip to look toward the sea? — A little cloud like a man’s hand rising out of the sea (1 Kings 18:44)

### 1 Kings 19
- Whose threat made Elijah run for his life to Beersheba? — Jezebel’s — she vowed to make his life like the prophets he had slain by that time the next day (1 Kings 19:2)
- What did Elijah pray under the broom tree? — “It is enough; now, O LORD, take away my life; for I am no better than my fathers” (1 Kings 19:4)
- What food did the angel provide Elijah, and how far did it carry him? — A cake baked on hot stones and a jar of water; in its strength he went forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the mount of God (1 Kings 19:6-8)
- At Horeb, the LORD was not in the wind, the earthquake, or the fire. Where was He? — In the still small voice (1 Kings 19:11-12)
- Name the three people Elijah was told to anoint. — Hazael as king over Syria, Jehu son of Nimshi as king over Israel, and Elisha son of Shaphat as prophet in his place (1 Kings 19:15-16)
- How many in Israel had not bowed the knee to Baal? — Seven thousand (1 Kings 19:18)
- What was Elisha doing when Elijah cast his mantle upon him? — Plowing with twelve yoke of oxen, and he was with the twelfth (1 Kings 19:19)

### 1 Kings 20
- How many kings joined Ben-hadad of Syria when he besieged Samaria? — Thirty-two (1 Kings 20:1)
- Complete Ahab’s reply to Ben-hadad’s boast: “Let not him that girds on his armor boast himself as he that ______.” — puts it off (1 Kings 20:11)
- Why did the Syrians think they lost the first battle, and what did they plan to do differently? — They said Israel’s gods were gods of the hills, so they planned to fight in the plain instead (1 Kings 20:23)
- How did Ben-hadad’s servants approach Ahab to plead for his life? — With sackcloth on their loins and ropes on their heads (1 Kings 20:31-32)

### 1 Kings 21
- Why did Naboth refuse to sell his vineyard to King Ahab? — “The LORD forbid that I should give you the inheritance of my fathers” (1 Kings 21:3)
- How did Jezebel arrange Naboth’s death? — She wrote letters in Ahab’s name, sealed with his seal, ordering a fast where two base fellows would charge Naboth with cursing God and the king, so he would be stoned (1 Kings 21:8-10)
- What did Ahab say when Elijah met him in Naboth’s vineyard? — “Have you found me, O my enemy?” (1 Kings 21:20)
- What did the LORD say would happen to Jezebel? — “The dogs shall eat Jezebel within the bounds of Jezreel” (1 Kings 21:23)
- Why did the LORD delay the evil on Ahab’s house until his son’s days? — Because Ahab humbled himself — he rent his clothes, put on sackcloth, and fasted (1 Kings 21:27-29)

### 1 Kings 22
- Before going to battle at Ramoth-gilead, what did Jehoshaphat ask Ahab to do first? — Inquire for the word of the LORD (1 Kings 22:5)
- Why did Ahab hate the prophet Micaiah the son of Imlah? — “He never prophesies good concerning me, but evil” (1 Kings 22:8)
- Which false prophet made horns of iron and said Israel would push the Syrians with them? — Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah (1 Kings 22:11)
- In Micaiah’s vision of the LORD’s throne, what kind of spirit volunteered to entice Ahab? — A lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets (1 Kings 22:21-22)
- Ahab disguised himself in battle, yet he was still killed. How? — A certain man drew his bow at a venture and struck him between the scale armor and the breastplate (1 Kings 22:34)
- Where did the dogs lick up Ahab’s blood, fulfilling the word of the LORD? — By the pool of Samaria, where they washed his chariot (1 Kings 22:38)

## 2 Kings

### 2 Kings 1
- How was King Ahaziah injured, and which false god did he send messengers to consult? — He fell through the lattice in his upper chamber in Samaria, and he sent to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron (2 Kings 1:2)
- When the messengers described a man wearing a garment of haircloth with a leather girdle, who did Ahaziah know it was? — Elijah the Tishbite (2 Kings 1:8)
- What happened to the first two captains of fifty sent to bring Elijah down, and how did the third captain act differently? — Fire came down from heaven and consumed each captain with his fifty men; the third fell on his knees and begged that his life be precious in Elijah’s sight (2 Kings 1:10-14)

### 2 Kings 2
- What did Elisha ask of Elijah before he was taken away? — “Let me inherit a double share of your spirit” (2 Kings 2:9)
- Describe how Elijah was taken up to heaven. — A chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them, and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven (2 Kings 2:11)
- What did Elisha say when he struck the Jordan with Elijah’s mantle? — “Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?” — and the water parted (2 Kings 2:14)
- How did Elisha heal the bad water at Jericho? — He threw salt from a new bowl into the spring, saying the LORD had made the water wholesome (2 Kings 2:20-21)
- What happened to the boys who jeered at Elisha, calling him “baldhead”? — He cursed them in the name of the LORD, and two she-bears came out of the woods and tore forty-two of them (2 Kings 2:23-24)

### 2 Kings 3
- What tribute did Mesha king of Moab, a sheep breeder, have to deliver annually to the king of Israel? — A hundred thousand lambs and the wool of a hundred thousand rams (2 Kings 3:4)
- What did Elisha ask for before the power of the LORD came upon him to prophesy? — A minstrel; when the minstrel played, the power of the LORD came upon him (2 Kings 3:15)
- Why did the Moabites rush carelessly toward the camp of Israel? — The rising sun made the water look as red as blood, and they thought the kings had slain one another (2 Kings 3:22-23)

### 2 Kings 4
- When the widow of one of the sons of the prophets was about to lose her two children to a creditor, what did Elisha tell her to borrow? — Empty vessels from all her neighbors — “and not too few” (2 Kings 4:3)
- What four pieces of furniture did the Shunammite woman put in the roof chamber for Elisha? — A bed, a table, a chair, and a lamp (2 Kings 4:10)
- When Elisha stretched himself upon the Shunammite’s dead son, what did the child do as he came back to life? — He sneezed seven times and opened his eyes (2 Kings 4:35)
- When the sons of the prophets cried “O man of God, there is death in the pot!”, what did Elisha throw in to make the pottage harmless? — Meal (2 Kings 4:40-41)
- With what did Elisha feed a hundred men, with some left over? — Twenty loaves of barley and fresh ears of grain, brought by a man from Baal-shalishah (2 Kings 4:42-44)

### 2 Kings 5
- Who first told Naaman’s household about the prophet in Samaria who could cure his leprosy? — The little maid from the land of Israel who had been carried off in a raid and served Naaman’s wife (2 Kings 5:2-3)
- What simple instruction from Elisha made Naaman angry at first? — “Go and wash in the Jordan seven times, and your flesh shall be restored” (2 Kings 5:10-12)
- Which two rivers of Damascus did Naaman claim were better than all the waters of Israel? — Abana and Pharpar (2 Kings 5:12)
- After Naaman dipped seven times in the Jordan, what was his flesh like? — It was restored like the flesh of a little child, and he was clean (2 Kings 5:14)
- What judgment fell on Gehazi for running after Naaman and taking silver and garments by deceit? — The leprosy of Naaman cleaved to him and his descendants forever; he went out a leper, as white as snow (2 Kings 5:27)

### 2 Kings 6
- Why was the son of the prophets so upset when his axe head fell into the Jordan, and how did Elisha recover it? — It was borrowed; Elisha threw a stick in the water and made the iron float (2 Kings 6:5-6)
- How did the king of Syria’s servant explain why Israel kept escaping his plans? — “Elisha, the prophet in Israel, tells the king of Israel the words that you speak in your bedchamber” (2 Kings 6:12)
- When Elisha’s servant panicked at the Syrian army surrounding Dothan, what did Elisha pray, and what did the servant see? — “O LORD, open his eyes that he may see” — and he saw the mountain full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha (2 Kings 6:16-17)
- During the siege of Samaria the famine grew so severe that an ass’s head sold for how much? — Eighty shekels of silver (2 Kings 6:25)

### 2 Kings 7
- What price did Elisha prophesy for fine meal and barley by the next day at the gate of Samaria? — A measure of fine meal for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel (2 Kings 7:1)
- What was the fate of the captain who doubted, saying, “If the LORD himself should make windows in heaven, could this thing be?” — He saw the plenty with his own eyes but did not eat of it — the people trod upon him in the gate and he died (2 Kings 7:2, 17)
- Who discovered that the Syrian camp was deserted, and why had the Syrians fled? — Four lepers at the gate; the Lord had made the Syrian army hear the sound of chariots and horses, and they fled for their lives (2 Kings 7:3-7)
- What did the lepers say to each other that made them stop hiding the good news? — “We are not doing right. This day is a day of good news; if we are silent… punishment will overtake us” (2 Kings 7:9)

### 2 Kings 8
- When the Shunammite woman returned after seven years of famine, who happened to be telling the king about her son being restored to life at that very moment? — Gehazi, the servant of Elisha (2 Kings 8:4-6)
- Why did Elisha weep as he stared at Hazael? — Because he knew the evil Hazael would do to the people of Israel — burning fortresses, slaying young men, and destroying little ones (2 Kings 8:11-12)
- How did Ben-hadad king of Syria die? — Hazael dipped a coverlet in water and spread it over his face until he died, and then reigned in his place (2 Kings 8:15)
- Why would the LORD not destroy Judah despite King Jehoram’s evil? — For the sake of David his servant, since He promised to give a lamp to him and his sons forever (2 Kings 8:19)

### 2 Kings 9
- Elisha sent one of the sons of the prophets to Ramoth-gilead with a flask of oil. What was his errand, and what was he to do right after? — To anoint Jehu king over Israel in an inner chamber, then open the door and flee without tarrying (2 Kings 9:1-3)
- How did the watchman on the tower in Jezreel recognize who was approaching? — “The driving is like the driving of Jehu the son of Nimshi; for he drives furiously” (2 Kings 9:20)
- On whose property did Jehu order Joram’s body to be thrown, and why? — On the plot of Naboth the Jezreelite, in fulfillment of the LORD’s word against Ahab (2 Kings 9:24-26)
- When they went to bury Jezebel, what was left of her? — Only the skull, the feet, and the palms of her hands — just as the LORD had spoken by Elijah (2 Kings 9:35-36)

### 2 Kings 10
- Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. What gruesome proof of loyalty did Jehu demand from the city’s leaders? — Their heads, which were sent in baskets to Jezreel and laid in two heaps at the gate (2 Kings 10:6-8)
- How did Jehu trick all the worshipers of Baal into one place? — He announced a great sacrifice to Baal, claiming “Ahab served Baal a little, but Jehu will serve him much,” then had them all slain and made the house of Baal a latrine (2 Kings 10:18-27)

### 2 Kings 11
- When Athaliah set out to destroy all the royal family, who rescued the baby Joash, and how? — Jehosheba, sister of Ahaziah, stole him away with his nurse and hid him in a bedchamber (2 Kings 11:2)
- Where was Joash hidden, and for how long, while Athaliah reigned? — In the house of the LORD for six years (2 Kings 11:3)
- What did Athaliah cry out when she saw the newly crowned king standing by the pillar? — “Treason! Treason!” (2 Kings 11:14)
- How old was Jehoash when he began to reign? — Seven years old (2 Kings 11:21)

### 2 Kings 12
- What did Jehoiada the priest make to collect money for repairing the house of the LORD? — A chest with a hole bored in the lid, set beside the altar (2 Kings 12:9)
- Why was no accounting asked from the men who paid the workmen repairing the temple? — Because they dealt honestly (2 Kings 12:15)

### 2 Kings 13
- When Elisha told the dying king Joash to strike the ground with the arrows, how many times did he strike, and why was Elisha angry? — Three times; he should have struck five or six times, and then he would have made an end of Syria (2 Kings 13:18-19)
- What happened to the dead man who was cast into Elisha’s grave? — As soon as he touched the bones of Elisha, he revived and stood on his feet (2 Kings 13:21)

### 2 Kings 14
- When Amaziah challenged him to battle, Jehoash king of Israel answered with a parable about two plants. Which two? — A thistle on Lebanon and a cedar on Lebanon (2 Kings 14:9)
- Jeroboam II restored the border of Israel according to the word of the LORD spoken by which prophet? — Jonah the son of Amittai, from Gath-hepher (2 Kings 14:25)

### 2 Kings 15
- What affliction did the LORD send on King Azariah, and how did he live because of it? — He was a leper to the day of his death and dwelt in a separate house, while his son Jotham governed (2 Kings 15:5)
- Zechariah reigned only six months before being struck down. What promise of the LORD did his short reign complete? — The promise to Jehu: “Your sons shall sit upon the throne of Israel to the fourth generation” (2 Kings 15:12)

### 2 Kings 16
- What abominable practice of the nations did King Ahaz commit with his own son? — He burned his son as an offering (2 Kings 16:3)
- What did Ahaz see in Damascus that he ordered Uriah the priest to copy in Jerusalem? — The altar at Damascus — he sent a model and its pattern, exact in all details (2 Kings 16:10-11)

### 2 Kings 17
- In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria. Where did he place the exiled Israelites? — In Halah, on the Habor (the river of Gozan), and in the cities of the Medes (2 Kings 17:6)
- What did the LORD send among the foreign settlers of Samaria because they did not fear Him, and what did the king of Assyria do about it? — Lions, which killed some of them; the king sent back one of the exiled priests to Bethel to teach them the law of the god of the land (2 Kings 17:25-28)
- What double-minded worship did the settler nations practice? — “They feared the LORD but also served their own gods” (2 Kings 17:33)

### 2 Kings 18
- Hezekiah broke in pieces the bronze serpent that Moses had made. Why, and what was it called? — The people of Israel had been burning incense to it; it was called Nehushtan (2 Kings 18:4)
- How does Scripture rank Hezekiah among the kings of Judah? — He trusted in the LORD so that there was none like him among all the kings of Judah, after him or before him (2 Kings 18:5)
- What did the Rabshakeh call Egypt when mocking Judah’s alliances? — “That broken reed of a staff, which will pierce the hand of any man who leans on it” (2 Kings 18:21)
- Why did Eliakim, Shebnah, and Joah ask the Rabshakeh to speak in Aramaic, and what did he do instead? — They didn’t want the people on the wall to understand; instead he called out even louder in the language of Judah (2 Kings 18:26-28)

### 2 Kings 19
- What did Hezekiah do with the threatening letter from Sennacherib’s messengers? — He went up to the house of the LORD and spread it before the LORD, then prayed (2 Kings 19:14)
- Complete the LORD’s word against the king of Assyria: “I will put my hook in your ______ and my bit in your ______, and I will turn you back on the way by which you came.” — nose … mouth (2 Kings 19:28)
- How many Assyrians did the angel of the LORD slay in the camp that night? — A hundred and eighty-five thousand (2 Kings 19:35)
- How did Sennacherib meet his end? — While worshiping in the house of Nisroch his god, his own sons Adrammelech and Sharezer slew him with the sword (2 Kings 19:37)

### 2 Kings 20
- When Hezekiah was sick and told to set his house in order, what moved the LORD to heal him, and how many years were added to his life? — The LORD heard his prayer and saw his tears; fifteen years were added (2 Kings 20:5-6)
- What sign confirmed that the LORD would heal Hezekiah? — The shadow went back ten steps on the dial of Ahaz (2 Kings 20:9-11)
- What did Hezekiah show the envoys from Babylon, and what did Isaiah prophesy because of it? — He showed them everything in his treasure house and storehouses; Isaiah prophesied that all of it would one day be carried to Babylon, and some of his own sons would serve in the palace of the king of Babylon (2 Kings 20:13-18)

### 2 Kings 21
- How old was Manasseh when he began to reign, and how long did he reign? — Twelve years old, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem (2 Kings 21:1)
- What vivid picture did the LORD give of the judgment coming on Jerusalem because of Manasseh? — “I will wipe Jerusalem as one wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it upside down” (2 Kings 21:13)

### 2 Kings 22
- How old was Josiah when he became king? — Eight years old (2 Kings 22:1)
- What did Hilkiah the high priest find in the house of the LORD, and what did the king do when it was read to him? — The book of the law; when Josiah heard its words, he rent his clothes (2 Kings 22:8-11)
- Which prophetess did Josiah’s officials consult about the words of the book? — Huldah, the wife of Shallum, keeper of the wardrobe, who dwelt in Jerusalem in the Second Quarter (2 Kings 22:14)

### 2 Kings 23
- When Josiah destroyed the altar at Bethel and burned bones upon it, whose ancient prophecy was fulfilled — and whose tomb did he order left untouched? — The prophecy of the man of God who came from Judah in Jeroboam’s day; Josiah said of his tomb, “Let him be; let no man move his bones” (2 Kings 23:15-18)
- What was special about the passover Josiah commanded? — No such passover had been kept since the days of the judges, or in all the days of the kings of Israel or Judah (2 Kings 23:21-22)
- How was Josiah killed? — Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt slew him at Megiddo (2 Kings 23:29)

### 2 Kings 24
- When Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem, King Jehoiachin gave himself up. Whom did the king of Babylon carry into captivity, and who was left? — Jehoiachin with his mother, wives, officials, and chief men, plus ten thousand captives including all the craftsmen and smiths; none remained except the poorest people of the land (2 Kings 24:12-14)
- Who did the king of Babylon make king in Jehoiachin’s place, and what new name did he give him? — Mattaniah, Jehoiachin’s uncle, renamed Zedekiah (2 Kings 24:17)

### 2 Kings 25
- What was the last thing Zedekiah saw before he was blinded? — The slaying of his sons before his eyes; then he was bound in fetters and taken to Babylon (2 Kings 25:7)
- Who burned the house of the LORD and all the great houses of Jerusalem, and who was left behind in the land? — Nebuzaradan, the captain of the bodyguard; he left some of the poorest of the land as vinedressers and plowmen (2 Kings 25:8-12)
- How does 2 Kings end — what kindness was shown to Jehoiachin in the thirty-seventh year of his exile? — Evil-merodach king of Babylon graciously freed him from prison, spoke kindly to him, gave him a seat above the other kings, and he dined at the king’s table every day of his life (2 Kings 25:27-30)
